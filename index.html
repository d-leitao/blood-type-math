About the numbers

Compatibility here covers ABO and RhD only. Hospitals still type and crossmatch every unit, and other antigen systems (Kell, Duffy, Kidd and more) matter for some patients. Plasma is matched on ABO alone.

Country figures come from national blood services as compiled in Blood type distribution by country on Wikipedia. Many are measured on donor populations rather than whole countries. Rows the source doesn’t total to 100% (the UK’s sums to 96%) are rescaled. Portugal’s figures trace to the Portuguese Blood and Transplantation Institute (IPST) and assume RhD is independent of ABO.
